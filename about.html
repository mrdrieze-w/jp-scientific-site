About JP Scientific

JP Scientific Ltd. grew out of the Pawliszyn Research Group at the University of Waterloo — the lab where Professor Janusz Pawliszyn developed Solid-Phase Microextraction (SPME) in the early 1990s, now one of the most widely cited sample preparation techniques in analytical chemistry.

We specialize in developing SPME sample preparation tools and workflows for rapid and clean extraction of compounds from complex matrices for analytical testing.

Our mission is to support analytical chemists and laboratory technicians with robust, high-performance sample preparation tools designed for maximum extraction efficiency, longevity, and reproducibility — across both LC-MS and GC-MS workflows.

Whether you are performing environmental monitoring, agricultural residue testing, food analysis, or pharmaceutical bioanalysis, JP Scientific delivers dependable products backed by decades of published, peer-reviewed research.
